Case study 03 / Peloton · Support leadership

From 10 agents to teams of 50+.

At Peloton, I led a hardware and software support team before moving into Operational Excellence. As my scope grew, I built training, coached teams, and improved how complex cases moved through support.

Support leadership

10Technical agents→50+Associates & specialists

Team development

Help agents get up to speed and grow.

As Hardware & Software Team Lead, I managed 10 technical support agents. I designed and ran new-hire training and worked with product managers to build troubleshooting workflows for the issues agents were handling.

As Senior Supervisor, Operational Excellence, I led and mentored teams of more than 50 associates and specialists across B2B and B2C support. I used tailored development plans to help people build skills and prepare for their next roles.

Team development
50% team promotion rate reported in my résumé.
Onboarding
20% shorter new-hire ramp-up time after designing and running training programs.
Technical support
95% SLA adherence as the senior escalation point for about 2,000 technical inquiries per month.

Operational excellence

Fix the issues that keep coming back.

I worked with Product, Engineering, and Customer Success to find the causes of recurring escalations. I also redesigned dispute workflows and used automated triage to help route cases.

The cross-functional work reduced recurring escalations by 35%. The workflow and triage changes improved dispute-resolution efficiency by 40%.

These are reported results from my résumé for separate initiatives during my time at Peloton.
