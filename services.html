Who it's for

  • Company sizeRoughly 10 to 500 employees, with a support team that has outgrown its setup.
  • IndustriesDTC brands, connected hardware, and SaaS, where orders, device troubleshooting, and account questions share one queue.
  • PlatformsZendesk, Gorgias, Salesforce (Service Cloud), or a comparable help desk, with or without an AI agent already live.
  • Who usually buysA Head of Support, CX lead, COO, or founder who wants an outside view before spending more on headcount or tools.

Problems it solves

  • Your AI agent is live, but the automated resolution rate has stalled. Customers loop, ask for a human, or get confident wrong answers.
  • The knowledge base has drifted. Articles contradict each other, miss top contact reasons, or were never written for an AI agent to read.
  • You cannot say what is driving ticket volume. Tags and fields grew over time and no longer tell you much.
  • Escalations bounce. Tickets move between tiers or an outsourced partner without clear ownership, so customers write in again.
  • You are about to hire or buy before fixing the system. You want to know what the current setup can do first.

What's included

  • 01

    Help desk and ticket-mix review

    Queues, views, routing, tags, macros, and SLAs, plus a recent ticket sample mapped by contact reason, channel, and effort.

  • 02

    Knowledge base gap analysis

    Your help center checked against your top contact reasons: what is missing, outdated, duplicated, or hard for customers and AI agents to use.

  • 03

    AI agent and automation configuration review

    The content your AI agent draws from, its intents or procedures, guardrails, handoff rules, and a sample of real AI conversations, plus the triggers and rules around it.

  • 04

    Escalation workflow review

    How tickets move to Tier 2, specialist teams, or an outsourced partner, where ownership gets unclear, and where customers end up contacting you again.

  • 05

    Prioritized roadmap

    Every finding ranked by effort and expected impact, split into quick wins (days), near-term fixes (weeks), and larger projects (quarter), each with an estimated effect on volume, resolution, or effort.

How it works

  1. Before kickoff

    Access and data

    You grant read-only access and share a recent ticket export (90 days is ideal), plus a short intake questionnaire.

  2. Days 1-2

    Kickoff and interviews

    A 60-minute kickoff, then short interviews with your support lead, a few frontline agents, and whoever owns escalations.

  3. Days 3-6

    Analysis

    Ticket-mix analysis, knowledge base gap review, and AI agent and automation configuration review.

  4. Days 7-8

    Escalations and roadmap

    Escalation mapping, then findings are scored and built into the roadmap.

  5. Days 9-10

    Readout and handoff

    Live readout, written report, and Q&A. You leave with an owner and next step for every quick win.

FAQ

Which help desks do you work with?

Zendesk, Gorgias, and Salesforce (Service Cloud) are the most common. If you use a comparable help desk, ask on the intro call and I will tell you plainly whether it is a good fit.

We have not launched an AI agent yet. Is this still useful?

Yes. The audit then focuses on readiness: which contact reasons are good candidates for automation, what the knowledge base needs first, and how handoffs to your team should work from day one.

Will you change anything in our help desk?

Not during the audit. The review is read-only. Changes happen in the Quick-Win Sprint, an implementation project, or by your own team using the roadmap.

How much of my team's time does it take?

Plan for a 60-minute kickoff, a few 30-to-45-minute interviews, and the readout. For most teams that is a few hours in total, spread across two weeks.

Can you guarantee a specific automated resolution rate?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Every impact figure in the roadmap is an estimate based on your own ticket data, and the report shows the reasoning behind it.

How do you handle our customer data?

I am happy to sign your NDA, I work with the lowest access level that gets the job done, and ticket exports are deleted at the end of the engagement.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, the audit is fully remote. For teams in Dallas-Fort Worth, an on-site kickoff or readout is available on request.

What happens after the readout?

Your team can run the roadmap on its own. If you want help, the Quick-Win Sprint, an implementation project, or a monthly retainer picks up where the audit ends.
