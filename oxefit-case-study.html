Case study 02 / OxeFit · Zendesk AI

From 32% to 65% automated resolution.

As Customer Experience Manager at OxeFit, I built and refined Zendesk AI support agents and launched the Help Center for customers using XS1, XP1, and OxeAI.

Automated resolution

Before
32%
After
65%

What changed

Give customers a way to help themselves.

Support covered three different product experiences, with questions about equipment, software, membership, and service. I wanted customers to have a practical way to find answers before waiting for an agent.

I launched the OxeFit Help Center from scratch as the primary technical writer. I structured the content and wrote help articles, troubleshooting guides, repair documentation, and release notes. In Zendesk, I designed, built, and optimized the AI agents used in the same support operation.

Scope
XS1, XP1, and OxeAI support across connected fitness and software.
My role
Customer Experience Manager, AI agent rollout lead, and primary Help Center writer.
Reported outcome
Automated resolution rose from 32% to 65%, a 33 percentage-point increase based on the reported rates.

The figures come from my résumé and portfolio reporting for this work across three product lines.

How I think about the metric

Did the customer get an answer?

When I evaluate support automation, I look at repeat contacts and customer satisfaction alongside automated resolution. If people have to come back with the same question, I want to know where the answer or the handoff fell short.
