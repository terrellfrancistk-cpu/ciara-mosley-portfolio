04 / How I work

A few things I believe.

  • 01

    Stay curious.

    Ask what someone is trying to do before deciding what to build.

  • 02

    Start with people.

    Make it easier to get help, whether it comes from a tool or a person.

  • 03

    Make the idea real.

    Put something in people’s hands early enough to learn from it.

  • 04

    Leave room to change.

    Listen when the work tells you your first idea needs another pass.
