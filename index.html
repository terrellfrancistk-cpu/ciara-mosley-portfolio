About

Operational rigor, hands-on execution

Customer Experience leader with 8+ years building and scaling support operations across connected fitness, AI-powered products, and B2B and B2C environments in SaaS / tech.

At OxeFit, Ciara owned end-to-end CX for three product experiences (XS1, XP1, and OxeAI) and built AI support agents on Zendesk that lifted automated resolution from 32% to 65%. At Peloton, she managed 10 technical support agents as Team Lead, then led teams of 50+ associates and specialists as Senior Supervisor, Operational Excellence.

She has managed team leads, supervisors/managers, and outsourced BPO teams, including a 50-person outsourced BPO support team for two years, across phone, email, chat, and social. She builds multi-tier teams, standardizes customer journeys, resolves executive-level escalations and disputes, and turns customer signal into product and process change.

Today she is Co-Founder & COO of TK Hub LLC, an app studio, and uses Claude and ChatGPT daily for research, documentation, and product work.
