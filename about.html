About / Ciara Mosley

I like finding out why.

I bring the same curiosity to a customer’s support ticket, a team’s workflow, and the first few minutes someone spends in a new app.

The leader / The builder

From support to product.

My background in design and psychology taught me to look closely at what people do and ask what they expected to happen. In customer experience, that often means looking beyond the ticket to the product or process that brought someone to support in the first place.

At Peloton, I helped teams grow through coaching, clearer escalation paths, and training that made the work easier to learn. At OxeFit, I built help content and Zendesk AI agents around the questions customers actually asked.

At TK Hub LLC (opens in a new tab), I bring that same perspective to product reviews, launch readiness, and support. I enjoy the moment a rough idea becomes something we can put in front of someone and learn from.

A moment from the work

What happens after someone reads the instructions?

At OxeFit, I led the customer experience work for the XS1 Self-Service Repair program. I wrote customer-facing repair instructions and helped build the process around them, including replacement parts, liability requirements, training, and remote technical support.

That work is a good example of why I look at the whole experience. A customer following a repair guide may still need a part or a person who can help. The next step has to make sense from where they’re standing.

I bring that same question to product reviews at TK Hub. If someone has read the screen and still needs help, where do they go next?
